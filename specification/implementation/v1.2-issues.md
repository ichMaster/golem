## v1.2 — World state and the rules

### GOLEM-005 — The world state: moves, pushes, steps and the win

**Description:**
`golem/core/world.py`: the frozen `World` and `move`, implementing rules 1–4 and 6 of ARCHITECTURE §World and
rules, with the step budget taken from the level's tier. Touches `golem/core/world.py`,
`golem/core/levels.py` (`budget_for`), `tests/test_world.py` and `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `World`, a frozen dataclass with:
  - `level`;
  - `robot` (a cell) and `boxes` (a frozenset of cells);
  - `steps` and `step_budget`;
  - `status`: `"playing"`, `"solved"` or `"lost"`;
  - `loss_reason`: `None`, `"steps"` or `"deadlock"`. `"instructions"` is set by the interpreter in v3.2,
    not here.
- `new_world(level, budget) -> World` takes the robot and the boxes from the level, starts at 0 steps, and
  takes `step_budget` from `budget.steps`. A level whose boxes all start on goals is `solved` at once.
- `budget_for(level_id, budgets) -> Budget` in `levels.py`: the budget of the id's tier. A tier missing from
  the budgets is a `BudgetsError` that names it.
- `move(world, direction) -> (World, bool)`. The direction is `"N"`, `"E"`, `"S"` or `"W"`; anything else is
  a `ValueError` (the interpreter turns it into a Lua error in v3.2). The move:
  - onto floor or a goal: the robot moves, and the result is `True`;
  - into a wall: nothing moves, and the result is `False`;
  - into a box: the box is pushed if the cell behind it, in the same direction, is floor or a goal with no
    box. The box moves there and the robot into its old cell, and the result is `True`. Otherwise nothing
    moves, and the result is `False`. One box at a time, never a pull.
- Every `move` on a `playing` world counts one step, whether or not anything moved.
- After the move the world is `solved` when every box is on a goal. Otherwise it is `lost` with reason
  `"steps"` when `steps` has reached `step_budget`. Solving on the last step of the budget is a win. The
  corner rule joins this order in GOLEM-006.
- A `move` on a `solved` or `lost` world returns the same world and `False`, and counts no step.
- Keep `move` a pure function over plain data. No I/O, no clock, no randomness.
- ARCHITECTURE.md §World and rules: name `step_budget` among the `World` fields, the `loss_reason` values,
  the precedence after a move (solved, then deadlock, then steps), and the `ValueError` for an invalid
  direction.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
`move` plays classic Sokoban on a `World`, counting steps against the tier's budget, and the level ends as
solved or lost on steps.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `World` is immutable, and `move` never changes its argument.
- [ ] **Tests** (`tests/test_world.py`, unit tests over levels parsed from strings):
  - a move onto floor and onto a goal;
  - a move into a wall (`False`, a step counted);
  - a push onto floor and onto a goal;
  - a refused push into a wall and into another box (`False`, a step counted, nothing moved);
  - the step count after a mix of moves;
  - the step budget: the move that reaches it loses on `"steps"`, and solving on that same move wins;
  - the win;
  - no move and no step after `solved` or `lost`;
  - an invalid direction;
  - `new_world` on a level that starts solved;
  - `budget_for` with a known tier and a missing tier.
- [ ] **Contract:** the rules, the `World` fields and the loss reasons are in ARCHITECTURE §World and rules,
      pinned by `tests/test_world.py`, in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint and tests green; `tests/test_core_imports.py` still passes.

### GOLEM-006 — The corner rule

**Description:**
Rule 5's deadlock: a push that leaves a box on a cell that is not a goal, with walls on two orthogonal
sides, loses the level. Touches `golem/core/world.py` and `tests/test_world.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- After every push, check only the box that was pushed, and only against walls (another box is not a
  wall). Walls on north and east, east and south, south and west, or west and north lose the level as
  `"deadlock"`, unless the cell is a goal.
- The order after a move becomes: solved, then deadlock, then steps.
- A box that starts the level in a corner is not a loss; the rule fires only after a push.
- Walls on opposite sides (north and south, or east and west) are not a corner.

**Dependencies:** GOLEM-005

**Expected result:**
Pushing a box into a corner that is not a goal ends the level as lost on deadlock, and no other position
does.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - the corner rule in all four orientations, off a goal (loss) and on a goal (no loss);
  - a box between two walls on opposite sides (no loss);
  - a box next to another box in a "corner" made of a wall and that box (no loss);
  - a box that starts in a corner (no loss until pushed);
  - a deadlock push on the last step of the budget (reason `"deadlock"`, not `"steps"`);
  - no move after the deadlock.
- [ ] **Contract:** rule 5 as written in ARCHITECTURE §World and rules, pinned by these tests. No change to the
      document beyond GOLEM-005's precedence note.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint and tests green.

### GOLEM-007 — The robot's view and the rendered map

**Description:**
What a program will see (`look`, `here`, `boxes_left`) and what a report will show (`render`), as pure
functions of a `World`. Touches `golem/core/world.py` and `tests/test_world_view.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `look(world, direction) -> str` returns exactly `"wall"`, `"box"`, `"goal"`, `"box_on_goal"` or
  `"empty"` for the neighbouring cell. An invalid direction is a `ValueError`. The map is closed, so a
  neighbour of the robot is always inside it.
- `here(world) -> str` returns exactly `"goal"` or `"empty"`.
- `boxes_left(world) -> int` counts the boxes not on goals.
- `render(world) -> list[str]`:
  - one Sokoban text line per map row;
  - `#` wall, `$` box, `*` box on a goal, `.` goal, `@` robot, `+` robot on a goal, and a space for floor
    and for anything outside the map;
  - each line without trailing spaces.
- Round trip: for a freshly parsed level, `render(new_world(level, budget))` equals the level's map lines,
  with comments and the blank lines around the map removed, `-` and `_` turned into spaces, and trailing
  spaces stripped.

**Dependencies:** GOLEM-005

**Expected result:**
The robot API's view (`look`, `here`, `boxes_left`) and the report's final map exist in the core, with the
exact strings of the robot API contract.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Tests** (`tests/test_world_view.py`):
  - `look` returns each of the five strings, in every direction;
  - `here` on a goal and off it, including after the robot walks onto a goal;
  - `boxes_left` before and after a box reaches a goal, and after it leaves the goal again;
  - `render` round-trips strings with every cell character, ragged rows and comments;
  - `render` round-trips every committed training level;
  - `render` after moves shows `+`, `*` and `@` in the right cells.
- [ ] **Contract:** the exact strings of `look` and `here` (ARCHITECTURE §Robot language, the robot API
      table) are pinned by `tests/test_world_view.py`. No document change is needed.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint and tests green; `tests/test_core_imports.py` still passes.
