# v3.1 — Issues

Issues for phase **v3.1 — The screen's data from the server, and ten levels** (version **v3 — Game screen:
the "Clay and stone" design**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v3.1 and the
contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous
phase (GOLEM-034 → **GOLEM-035…037**).

The phase gives the designed screen of v3.2 everything it draws:
- `inside` with every WebSocket state;
- a `levels` message with each training level's start map;
- ten training levels across tiers 1 and 2.

It builds on the v2.1 WebSocket and the v1 core. It leaves the page itself to v3.2 and v3.3, and the
training levels of tier 3 and the hidden sets to v7.1.

**Reconciled with the code (2026-11-23):**
- **`Level.inside`** (`golem/core/level.py`) holds every non-wall cell that the robot's start reaches, with
  boxes not blocking. Walls are never in it. The handoff's renderer (`model()` in `golem/screen/art/golem-art.js`)
  treats a wall as not inside too, so the two agree.
- **`render(world)`** (`golem/core/world.py`) strips trailing spaces from each row. Every inside cell has
  a wall somewhere to its east on its own row (the parser's flood fill refuses an open map). So a rendered
  row always ends at its last wall, and its length never changes during a game. The mask can therefore be
  computed once per level, with exactly the lengths of `render`'s rows.
- **`GET /api/levels`** is built inline in `golem/server/app.py` (`_install_level_routes`). Its entry is
  `{id, tier, width, height, budget: {steps, instructions}}`. A broken level gives `500 level_error`, and a
  broken `budgets.toml` gives `500 budgets_error`.
- **`/ws`** is `golem/server/ws.py`:
  - `handle(text, game, levels_dir)` is a pure function from one message and the game to the reply and the
    next game;
  - `state_message(game)` builds the state;
  - an unknown type answers `unknown message type; use load, move or restart`.
- **Tests that pin the committed levels:**
  - `tests/test_committed_levels.py` requires one box per tier-one level and checks that t1-01…t1-03 are
    listed;
  - `tests/test_solutions.py` requires exactly one recorded solution per training level, replayed to
    `solved` within the budget;
  - both iterate `list_levels("training")`, so new levels are covered as soon as they exist.
- **The tier definitions:**
  - VISION.md §Glossary: tier 1 is one box in an open room, tier 2 several boxes and corridors.
  - `budgets.toml` gives tier 1 200 steps and tier 2 1000. Budgets are per tier, so the per-level step
    counts in the handoff's samples (250, 300, 400) do not apply.
- **The handoff's candidate maps** (`GolemClayScreen.dc.html`, `LV`):
  - t1-04 and t1-05 have two and three boxes, so as tier-one levels they need one box each;
  - t2-01…t2-05 are tier-two candidates as they stand, if they are solvable under the corner rule.
- **The fixture hidden level** `tests/fixtures/hidden/tier1/h-01` shares its border row `#########` with
  some candidate maps. A leak test must look for its distinctive rows, not for every row.
- **No earlier review left a finding homed to v3.1.** v2.4 review #3 is homed to v3.2.

## v3.1 — The screen's data from the server, and ten levels

### GOLEM-035 — Ten training levels: t1-04, t1-05 and t2-01…t2-05, each with a recorded solution

**Description:**
The training set grows from three levels to ten:
- the committed t1-01…t1-03 stay as they are;
- t1-04 and t1-05 join tier 1;
- t2-01…t2-05 make tier 2.

Each new level gets a recorded solution, which replays to `solved` within its tier's step budget. The
files are under `levels/training/` and `tests/fixtures/solutions/training/`, with `levels/README.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **t1-04 and t1-05:** one box each, in an open room (VISION's tier 1). Their room shapes may follow the
  handoff's candidates, with the extra boxes and goals removed.
- **t2-01…t2-05:**
  - several boxes, with walls inside the room that make corridors (VISION's tier 2);
  - taken from the handoff's candidates (`GolemClayScreen.dc.html`, `LV`; t2-03 is `MAPS.p`) where they
    parse and are solvable under the corner rule;
  - a candidate that is not solvable is adjusted, and the change is noted in the level's comment line.
- **Every level file** starts with a `;` comment that names the level and what it asks of the player, as
  t1-01…t1-03 do.
- **The recorded solutions:**
  - found with a throwaway search outside the repository, which runs the core's `move`;
  - written as `tests/fixtures/solutions/training/<tier>/<name>.txt`;
  - a solution need not be the shortest, but must fit the tier's step budget (tier 1: 200, tier 2: 1000).
- **`levels/README.md`:**
  - the sources table lists every new level, with its source (written for this project, or adapted from
    the design handoff's sample) and its licence (MIT, as the repository);
  - the tier 2 line no longer says "from v7.1": tier 2 starts here and grows in v7.1.
- **`tests/test_committed_levels.py`:**
  - the check of the first three becomes a check that the training set is exactly the ten ids;
  - a new test requires more than one box on every tier-two level, beside the one-box test of tier 1.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
The training set lists ten levels, and every one replays its recorded solution to `solved` through the
core.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `list_levels("training")` gives exactly t1-01…t1-05 and t2-01…t2-05, sorted.
- [ ] Every new level parses, and its boxes equal its goals.
  - Tier-one levels have one box each; tier-two levels have more than one.
- [ ] Every new level has a recorded solution that `tests/test_solutions.py` replays to `solved`, twice,
  with identical JSON, within the tier's step budget.
- [ ] `levels/README.md` lists the source and licence of all ten levels.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - the ten-id test;
  - the tier-two box-count test;
  - the existing solution, parse and save/restore tests now run over ten levels.
- [ ] **Gates:** `uv run ruff check .` clean, `uv run pytest` green.

### GOLEM-036 — Every WebSocket state carries `inside`, from the core

**Description:**
The `state` message gains `inside`.
- It is one string per row of `map`, as long as that row: `1` for a cell inside the walls, `0` for any
  other cell, walls included.
- The core computes it from `Level.inside`, so the page draws the floor only there and never computes
  it.
- The files: `golem/core/world.py` (or `level.py`), `golem/server/ws.py`, the WebSocket tests and the
  ARCHITECTURE contract.

**What needs to be done:**
- **A pure core function,** for example `inside_rows(level) -> list[str]`:
  - row `r` is as long as `render`'s row `r` (up to and including the row's last wall, `""` for a row
    with none);
  - `1` where `(r, c)` is in `level.inside`, `0` elsewhere;
  - it imports only the standard library, as the rest of `golem/core`.
- **`state_message`** adds `"inside"` after `"map"`. The mask is the level's, so it is the same for every
  state of a game.
- **Unchanged:**
  - the save file (`data/human-game.json`) stays the core's save format;
  - `inside` is not saved, because it is computed from the level on restore.
- **The text page** (v2.2) ignores the new field, so it keeps working until v3.2 replaces it.
- **ARCHITECTURE §HTTP API, WebSocket `/ws`:** the state message lists `inside` and its exact shape.
- **§The game screen** says the renderer draws the floor only where `inside` is `1`.
- **The brief** (`specification/design/game-screen-brief.md`) says the shape was "fixed when the screen is
  built (v3.1)". It gets a one-line pointer to the contract.

**Dependencies:** GOLEM-035

**Expected result:**
Every state that `/ws` sends, on connect, after `load`, `move` and `restart`, and after a restored
restart, carries `inside` that matches `Level.inside` cell for cell.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] For every training level, at the start and after a scripted sequence of moves, pushes included:
  - `len(state["inside"]) == len(state["map"])`;
  - each mask row is as long as its map row;
  - `inside[r][c] == "1"` exactly when `(r, c)` is in `Level.inside`.
- [ ] A wall is `0`, and a cell outside the walls (a leading space of an irregular tier-two row) is `0`.
- [ ] The state sent on connect and the state after a server restart restore carry the same `inside` as
  after `load`.
- [ ] `golem/core` still imports only the standard library (`tests/test_core_imports.py`).
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - plain-data tests of `inside_rows` on a hand-made irregular level;
  - in-process WebSocket tests (`TestClient`) over every training level;
  - the existing tests that compare whole state dicts are updated.
- [ ] **Contract:**
  - the WebSocket state message gains `inside`;
  - ARCHITECTURE §HTTP API (WebSocket `/ws`) and §The game screen are updated;
  - pinned by the new WebSocket tests.
- [ ] **Gates:** `uv run ruff check .` clean, `uv run pytest` green.

### GOLEM-037 — The `levels` WebSocket message: the training levels with their start maps

**Description:**
A new WebSocket message for the level picker. The client sends `{"type": "levels"}`, and the server
answers `{"type": "levels", "levels": [...]}`:
- one entry per training level, sorted by id;
- each entry has the fields of `GET /api/levels`, plus the start `map` and its `inside`;
- hidden levels are never listed.

`GET /api/levels` keeps its contract, with no map. The files: `golem/server/ws.py`,
`golem/server/app.py`, a shared helper, the tests and the ARCHITECTURE contract.

**What needs to be done:**
- **One builder for the entries** (for example in a small `golem/server/levels.py`), used by
  `GET /api/levels` and by the `levels` reply:
  - the reply adds `map` and `inside` to each entry;
  - the route stays byte-for-byte what it is today.
- **`handle`:**
  - answers `{"type": "levels"}` with the list, whether a game is loaded or not, and leaves the game as
    it was;
  - extra fields in the request are ignored.
- **The start map** is `render(new_world(level, budget))`, and `inside` is the mask of GOLEM-036.
- **A training level or a `budgets.toml` that does not load:**
  - the reply is `{"type": "error", "detail": "the level list cannot be loaded"}`;
  - the cause is logged, as `load` does;
  - the connection stays open.
- **The unknown-type error** names the new type: `unknown message type; use load, move, restart or
  levels`.
- **ARCHITECTURE §HTTP API, WebSocket `/ws`:**
  - the client messages list `levels`, and the reply shape is given with one example entry;
  - the text says why the map travels only over the WebSocket: `GET /api/levels` also serves
    `golem levels` (v5.1), and the agent must not get a level's map before it runs the level, while the
    agent never uses the screen's channel.

**Dependencies:** GOLEM-036

**Expected result:**
A scripted WebSocket client asks for `levels` and receives every training level with its start map and
mask, matching the level files and the core, and nothing about hidden levels.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The reply lists exactly `list_levels("training")`, in order, each entry with:
  - `id`, `tier`, `width`, `height` and `budget` equal to `GET /api/levels`;
  - `map` equal to `render` of the level's start;
  - `inside` equal to `inside_rows(level)`.
- [ ] Against `root_with_hidden`:
  - no entry has a hidden id;
  - no distinctive row of the fixture hidden map appears anywhere in the reply's JSON;
  - `GET /api/levels` still has no `map` and no `inside`, and no hidden id.
- [ ] `levels` before any `load` gets the list, and after it the game is unchanged.
- [ ] A broken training level gets the `error` reply and the connection stays open.
- [ ] Two fresh servers give byte-identical `levels` replies.
- [ ] **Tests:** in-process WebSocket tests (`TestClient`) for each criterion above, plus the unchanged
  `/api/levels` tests.
- [ ] **Contract:**
  - the WebSocket gains the `levels` request and reply;
  - the unknown-type detail changes;
  - ARCHITECTURE §HTTP API (WebSocket `/ws`) is updated;
  - pinned by the new tests.
- [ ] **Gates:** `uv run ruff check .` clean, `uv run pytest` green.
