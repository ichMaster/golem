### GOLEM-036: `inside` in every state
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 95bb285 · **GitHub:** #36
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests (897) · [ ] determinism (n/a: no interpreter change)
- **The core function:** `golem/core/world.py` gains `inside_rows(level)`.
  - It gives one string per rendered row: `1` for a cell in `level.inside`, `0` for a wall or a cell
    outside the walls.
  - Each row ends at the row's last wall, so the mask has the shape of `render`'s rows in every state.
- **The state:** every WebSocket `state` carries `inside` after `map`. The save file is unchanged, and a
  restored game computes the mask from its level.
- **Tests:**
  - a plain-data level with leading spaces, a blank row and a closed room the robot cannot reach;
  - every committed level against `Level.inside`;
  - every state of every training level over the WebSocket: load, each move of its recorded solution, and
    restart;
  - the state on connect and after a restore.
- **Mutation checks:** a mask as wide as the level, or one that marks every non-wall cell, fails 6 and 12
  tests.

### GOLEM-037: The `levels` message
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 733b631 · **GitHub:** #37
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests (905) · [x] determinism: two fresh servers give byte-identical replies.
- **The builder:** `golem/server/levels.py` (`training_levels`) builds the entries for `GET /api/levels`
  and for the WebSocket reply.
  - The route's three responses are byte-identical to before: the list, `500 level_error` and
    `500 budgets_error`. They were captured and compared before and after the change.
- **The reply:** `{"type": "levels"}` gets every training level, sorted by id, with the route's fields plus
  the start `map` and `inside`.
  - It works with or without a loaded game, and it neither changes nor saves the game.
  - A broken level or `budgets.toml` gets `the level list cannot be loaded`.
- **The leak test:** it uses only the fixture hidden map's rows that no training map shares. The border
  `#########` and the row `#       #` also appear in training maps.
- **Mutation checks:** listing the hidden set, or a mask of all ones, each fails the matching test.
- **Claude's live check** (a scratch server on 127.0.0.1:8711, with the `websockets` client):
  - `/api/levels` lists ten levels with no map;
  - `levels` answers ten entries;
  - a loaded t2-03 state carries `inside`;
  - a hidden id gets `unknown level`.
